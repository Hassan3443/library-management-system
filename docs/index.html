Welcome to City Library

Search the catalog, check availability, borrow and return books, reserve titles that are on loan, and keep track of your due dates and fines, all in one place.

Browse the catalog Go to my dashboard

What you can do

Search and discover

Find books by title, author or category, and see the live availability of every copy before you make a request. (FR-03, FR-04)

Borrow and return

Submit borrow requests, follow their approval, and return books when you are done. Loans run for 14 days. (FR-05, FR-08, FR-14, FR-15)

Stay on top of due dates

Your dashboard shows current loans, due dates, overdue notices and any fines, at 5 EGP per overdue day. (FR-06, FR-10, FR-11, FR-16)

Reserve popular books

If every copy is on loan, join the reservation queue and get notified when a copy comes back. (FR-09, FR-18)

Track your history

Your full borrowing history stays available, including return dates and fines from late returns. (FR-07)

Librarian tools

Librarians manage the catalog, copies, borrow requests, returns, overdue fines, members and reservation queues. (FR-12 to FR-18)
